What are the top health benefits of drinking coffee?

Your brew gives you benefits beyond an energy boost. Here are the top ways coffee can positively impact your health:

1. Your liver will thank you.

Coffee has a protective effect on the liver. Research shows that coffee drinkers are more likely to have liver enzyme levels within a healthy range than people who do not drink coffee. Research also shows an inverse association between coffee consumption and hepatocellular carcinoma risk. “This means that drinking more coffee of any type, including decaffeinated and instant coffee, reduces the risk of this type of cancer, as well as chronic liver disease,” Judd explains. This risk reduction is estimated at 41%–53%.

2. Your body may process glucose (sugar) better.

That’s the theory behind studies that found that people who drink coffee are less likely to develop type 2 diabetes. Coffee beans contain compounds called polyphenols that help lower inflammation and provide antioxidant benefits. These compounds can help improve the body’s insulin response, leading to better blood sugar control over time. “Polyphenols are found in both caffeinated and decaf beans, but the roast matters because heat degrades these micronutrients,” Judd explains. Darker roasts have less polyphenols while lighter roasts retain more. “It is important to remember that added sugar increases blood sugar and decreases insulin sensitivity, counteracting the positive effects of polyphenols,” Judd cautions. Therefore, choosing coffee with low or no added sugar will maximize these benefits.
