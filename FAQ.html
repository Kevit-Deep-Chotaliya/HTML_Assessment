What is the difference between Arabica and Robusta?

How much caffeine is in a cup of coffee?

An average cup contains about 95 mg of caffeine. Decaf coffee has much less, typically around 2 mg.

Does dark roast have more caffeine than light roast?

No. Light and dark roasts have very similar caffeine levels, though dark roasts often taste bolder.
